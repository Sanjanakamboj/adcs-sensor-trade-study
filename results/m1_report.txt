6. WRITTEN ENGINEERING CONCLUSIONS
==============================================================================
  Q: Why can a star tracker provide full instantaneous 3-axis attitude
     information?
  A: It does not measure a single line-of-sight vector — it triangulates
     many catalog star positions in its focal plane simultaneously and
     solves directly for a full attitude quaternion/DCM. Under the
     isotropic simplification used here, its information matrix is
     J_star = (1/sigma^2) I_3, full rank by construction.

  Q: Why can a single Sun sensor not?
  A: A Sun sensor outputs one body-frame unit vector. Its measurement
     Jacobian H = -skew(v_b) has rank 2 (skew-symmetric matrices of a
     nonzero vector are always rank-deficient by exactly one): rotation
     of the body about the Sun-line itself changes nothing measurable.

  Q: Why can a single magnetometer not?
  A: Same argument as the Sun sensor — it is also a single unit-vector
     measurement, so rotation about the local field-line direction is
     unobservable from that one reading.

  Q: Under what geometry does Sun + magnetometer recover full attitude?
  A: Whenever the two vectors are non-collinear, the stacked 6x3 Jacobian
     reaches rank 3, recovering full 3-axis instantaneous information.
     Conditioning is BEST near 90 deg separation.

  Q: When does that architecture become poorly conditioned?
  A: As the Sun-magnetic separation angle approaches 0 deg or 180 deg,
     the condition number of J grows without bound; at exactly 0/180 deg
     the geometry is singular (rank drops to 2, min singular value -> 0).

  Q: Which architecture has more uniform instantaneous attitude accuracy
     under the simplified assumptions?
  A: The star tracker: its isotropic model gives attitude information that
     is, by construction, independent of geometry/attitude. The Sun+mag
     architecture's accuracy varies strongly (and can degrade sharply)
     with the instantaneous Sun-magnetic separation angle.

  Q: What does this analysis NOT yet say?
  A: Nothing here addresses mass, power, cost, eclipse/Sun-exclusion
     availability duty cycle, blinding/stray light, update-rate effects
     on a real closed-loop control bandwidth, or full estimator (EKF)
     performance with gyro propagation and measurement history. Those are
     explicitly DEFERRED to a later milestone.
